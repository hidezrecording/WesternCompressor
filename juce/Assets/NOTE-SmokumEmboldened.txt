Modification (2026-10-14, for the Hotdog faceplate): every glyph
outline was expanded with a FreeType stroker at a radius of 72
font units = 3.5% of the 2048-unit em (a single stroked outline
pass: the original contours plus the stroker's band contours,
composed under the TrueType non-zero winding fill rule, so each
letterform gains real outline weight on all sides). Advance
widths are unchanged from Smokum. Family renamed to "Smokum
Emboldened" so the derivative can never be confused with the
original font.
