Modification (2026-10-14, for the Hotdog faceplate, GUI v26): the
same bake as SmokumEmboldened-Regular.ttf (every glyph outline
expanded with a FreeType stroker, original contours plus the
stroker's band contours composed under the TrueType non-zero
winding fill rule) but at a FURTHER REDUCED stroker radius of 29
font units = 1.42% of the 2048-unit em — down from 48 units
(2.34%, the v25 "Thin" bake) and 72 units (3.5%, the v24 bake).
Nathan found the v25 weight still a little too thick and said
the lettering "can still thin out a little bit and be okay", so
v26 deliberately bakes below the cautious band and verifies
legibility on the finished panel (solid-core gate, see
docs/build-report-v26.md). Advance widths are unchanged from
Smokum. Family renamed to "Smokum Emboldened XThin" so the
derivative can never be confused with the original font or with
the v24/v25 bakes. In v26 this bake serves ALL panel text AND
the flange numerals (v24/v25 kept a heavier bake for the
numerals; Nathan's v26 note said "all fonts").
