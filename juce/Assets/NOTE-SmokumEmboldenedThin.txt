Modification (2026-10-14, for the Hotdog faceplate, GUI v25): the
same bake as SmokumEmboldened-Regular.ttf (every glyph outline
expanded with a FreeType stroker, original contours plus the
stroker's band contours composed under the TrueType non-zero
winding fill rule) but at a REDUCED stroker radius of 48 font
units = 2.34% of the 2048-unit em, down from 72 units (3.5%) —
Nathan found the v24 weight a touch too bold and asked for a
slightly thinner line at slightly larger sizes. Advance widths
are unchanged from Smokum. Family renamed to "Smokum Emboldened
Thin" so the derivative can never be confused with the original
font or with the v24 bake (which the flange numerals still use).
